tonyshu at mit dot edu

I specialize in robotics, biomechanics, and neural control of movement. I previously led development of the MIT osseointegrated mechanoneural prosthesis. My work with the Biomechatronics Group demonstrated how amputated muscles can be configured to exceed the maximum movement speed of an intact limb.

More recently, I've been living aboard and refitting a sailboat to experience our changing climate up close and the progress of AI from a distance. Highlights include being marooned for months, learning to live without AC, and contemplating big questions regarding techno-religion.

I also offer academic consultations to professors on robotics research, but only when I'm not reading Byung-Chul Han or laying up fiberglass in the heat of the Caribbean sun.
